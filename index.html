FAQ

Questions from the vault.

What is Vaultra?

Vaultra is a free-to-play mobile game built around a command-deck vault. Play Code Harvest and the daily Vault Word, stack KeyCards, and climb the leaderboards.

What are KeyCards (KC)?

KeyCards are Vaultra's signature in-game item. Stack them by clearing quests, climbing the leaderboards, keeping your streak alive, and more. KC unlock Mythic items like the AI Agent. KeyCards are simulated in-game items and stay in the game.

What are Codes?

Codes are the in-game store currency. Every point you score becomes a Code, and the 7-day streak adds more. Spend them on wallpapers, themes and processor chips that shorten your cooldowns, built for real grinders.

Which platforms?

Vaultra is coming soon to Android (Google Play) and as a Telegram Mini App. Both platforms get Code Harvest and Vault Word, each with its own leaderboards.

How do I join the beta?

Sign up at vaultra.fun/signup with the Google account on your Android phone. We'll email your Google Play testing invite when testing opens.

Is it free to play?

Yes. Vaultra is free to play, and rewarded ads are always optional. You choose when to watch.

When does Vault Word reset?

Every day at 3:00 AM Eastern. The daily leaderboards close at the same time, and each day's top 3 get a temporary processor chip for the next day.
