Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Vaultra collects through vaultra.fun and the Vaultra beta signup, and how we use it. We keep it simple: we only ask for what we need to run the beta.

What we collect

When you join the beta list at vaultra.fun/signup, we collect the email address you enter (your Google Play / Gmail address). We do not ask for your name, phone number, location, or payment details.

Signup forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service. Like any website, our host (GitHub Pages) and font provider (Google Fonts) may receive standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site.

How we use your email

  • To invite you to the Vaultra Google Play Closed Beta. Google Play testing invites are tied to the Google account on your Android phone, which is why we ask for that email.
  • To send beta updates, testing instructions, and launch news about Vaultra.
  • To reply when you contact support.

We do not sell your data

We never sell, rent, or trade your email address or any personal information. We only share it with the services needed to run the beta, such as our form and email providers and Google Play (to add you as a tester).

Unsubscribe anytime

You can leave the list at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any email from us, or email vaultra@mail.grokbot.com and ask to be removed. You can also ask us to delete your email address and we will do so.

How long we keep it

We keep your email for as long as you are on the beta or launch list. If you unsubscribe or ask for deletion, we remove it from our list.

Children

The Vaultra beta is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as Vaultra grows. We will post changes on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: vaultra@mail.grokbot.com
